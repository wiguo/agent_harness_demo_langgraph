# Customer Complaint Handling Policy

*Fictional company: Northwind Retail Ltd. Version 2.3, effective 1 March 2026.*

## Definition of a complaint

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction from a customer about our products,
services, staff, or processes, whether or not a remedy is requested. A routine question or a
first-time service request is not a complaint; it becomes one if the customer indicates the
initial handling was unsatisfactory.

## Complaint categories

Every complaint must be classified into exactly one of five categories at intake:

1. **Billing** — incorrect charges, duplicate payments, refund delays, pricing disputes.
2. **Delivery** — late, lost, or damaged shipments; failed delivery attempts.
3. **Product Quality** — defective or misdescribed products, early failures, safety concerns.
4. **Service** — staff conduct, long wait times, unhelpful or incorrect advice.
5. **Data/Privacy** — misuse of personal data, unwanted marketing, access-request issues.

Category-specific handling: Billing complaints must be reconciled against the payment ledger
before any response is sent. Delivery complaints require a carrier trace reference. Product
Quality complaints involving a safety concern are always severity Tier 3. Service complaints
are reviewed by the team lead of the staff member involved. Data/Privacy complaints must be
copied to the Privacy Officer on the day of intake.

## Severity tiers

- **Tier 1 (Standard):** no financial loss above £100, no regulatory exposure.
- **Tier 2 (Significant):** financial loss £100–£1,000, repeat complaint, or vulnerable customer.
- **Tier 3 (Critical):** regulatory or legal exposure, safety issue, any Data/Privacy breach,
  or financial loss above £1,000.

## Escalation rules

A complaint unresolved **48 hours** after intake escalates automatically to Tier 2 and is
assigned to a senior agent. Tier 3 complaints escalate immediately to the Complaints Manager
and, where the complaint is regulatory or involves personal data, to the Privacy Officer the
same business day. Any complaint open beyond 10 business days is reported to the Head of
Customer Operations weekly.

## Response time standards by channel

- **Phone:** acknowledge immediately; substantive response within 24 hours.
- **Chat:** acknowledge in-session; substantive response within 24 hours.
- **Email:** acknowledge within 4 business hours; substantive response within 48 hours.
- **Web Form:** acknowledge within 8 business hours; substantive response within 48 hours.

## Ownership

The agent who records a complaint owns it until resolution or escalation. Ownership
transfers must be explicit — a complaint may never be left unassigned. The owner is
responsible for keeping the case record current, including category, severity, and status.

## Resolution and closure

A complaint may be closed only when: the customer has received a substantive response; any
agreed remedy has been actioned; and the case record shows category, severity, root cause,
and resolution time in hours. Cases are marked **resolved** when the remedy is delivered and
**closed** after a 5-business-day confirmation window with no further customer contact.
