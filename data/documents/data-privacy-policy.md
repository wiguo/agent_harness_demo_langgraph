# Data Privacy Policy

*Fictional company: Northwind Retail Ltd. Version 3.1, effective 1 March 2026.*

## Categories of customer data

We hold four categories of customer data: identity and contact details; order and payment
history; service interaction records (including complaint cases and call recordings); and
marketing preferences. Each category has a named data owner and a documented lawful basis.

## Retention periods

- **Complaint records:** retained for **24 months** from case closure, then anonymised.
- **Call recordings:** retained for **90 days**, then deleted, unless attached to an open
  Tier 3 complaint or a legal hold.
- **Order and payment history:** 6 years, to meet financial record-keeping obligations.
- **Marketing preferences:** for the life of the account, reviewed on any consent change.

## Access control and least privilege

Access to customer data is role-based and granted on a least-privilege basis: agents see
only the cases assigned to their team; exports of more than 50 records require Privacy
Officer approval; production data is never copied into test systems. Access rights are
reviewed quarterly and revoked on role change the same day.

## Third-party sharing

Customer data is shared only with processors under written contract (delivery carriers,
payment processors, the cloud hosting provider) and never sold. Each processor is listed in
the processor register with the data categories shared and the retention applied.

## Subject access requests

Customers may request a copy of their data at any time. Requests are logged as Data/Privacy
cases, acknowledged within 3 business days, and fulfilled within 30 calendar days. Identity
must be verified before any data is released.

## Breach notification

A suspected personal-data breach is reported to the Privacy Officer within 24 hours of
discovery. Where the breach is likely to result in risk to customers, the supervisory
authority is notified within 72 hours, and affected customers are informed without undue
delay with a description of the breach and the steps taken.
