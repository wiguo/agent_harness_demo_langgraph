# Customer Service Guidelines

*Fictional company: Northwind Retail Ltd. Version 1.8, effective 1 March 2026.*

## Communication principles

Be clear, honest, and specific. Use plain language; avoid internal jargon and system names.
Never promise what you cannot personally ensure will happen. If you don't know, say so and
commit to a time by which you will find out. Every customer interaction is logged in the
case record the same day.

## Greeting and closing standards

Open every interaction by introducing yourself by first name and confirming the customer's
issue in your own words before acting on it. Close by summarising what was done, what happens
next, and when. On phone and chat, ask whether anything else is needed before ending.

## Tone and professionalism

Stay calm and courteous regardless of the customer's tone. Do not argue, assign blame to
colleagues, or speculate about company decisions. Empathy first, process second: acknowledge
the inconvenience before explaining the fix. Written responses are proofread before sending.

## When to escalate versus resolve

Resolve at first contact when the remedy is within your authority (refunds up to £100,
redelivery, replacement of items under £250). Escalate when the customer requests it, when
the remedy exceeds your authority, when the complaint is Tier 2 or above, or when the same
customer has complained about the same issue twice. Escalating early is never penalised;
letting a case go stale is.

## Service recovery

When we are at fault, follow the three-step recovery: (1) a specific apology naming what
went wrong, (2) a concrete remedy with a date, (3) a follow-up contact after the remedy to
confirm satisfaction. High-priority recovery applies to Billing and Delivery complaints —
the two most common categories — where a same-week goodwill gesture is authorised without
manager approval.

## Channel-specific standards

- **Phone:** answer within 60 seconds where possible; offer a callback rather than holds
  over 5 minutes.
- **Chat:** first response within 2 minutes; never leave a chat idle over 3 minutes without
  an update.
- **Email:** match the customer's level of formality; one topic per paragraph; always
  include the case reference.
- **Web Form:** respond by email; restate the submitted issue since the customer cannot see
  their original text.
